**Short answer:** App Store app previews are 15–30 seconds long, H.264 (or ProRes) in .mov, .m4v or .mp4, at up to 30 frames per second, and each iPhone and iPad display size has its own resolution. For the current largest iPhones (6.9-inch), portrait previews are 886×1920. You can add up to three previews per localization.

Apple updates these specs from time to time, so check App Store Connect's _app preview specifications_ before you upload. The table below covers the sizes most apps need.

## Common resolutions

| Display          | Portrait  | Landscape |
| ---------------- | --------- | --------- |
| iPhone 6.9″      | 886×1920  | 1920×886  |
| iPhone 6.5″      | 886×1920  | 1920×886  |
| iPhone 5.5″      | 1080×1920 | 1920×1080 |
| iPad 13″ / 12.9″ | 1200×1600 | 1600×1200 |

## What App Review checks

Apple wants previews to show the app itself. In practice:

- **Use footage of your app's real UI.** Screen captures or screens shown as they appear in the app.
- **Avoid hands, people using devices, and heavy device frames.**
- **Don't promise what the app doesn't do,** and keep text overlays short.
- **The first frames matter twice:** previews autoplay muted in search results and on your product page.

That's why stylized promo videos (spinning devices, glitch cuts) are great for social posts but risky as App Store previews. Use a quiet style and let the screens carry it.

## Making one

1. Record your app on a device or simulator (QuickTime and Xcode both record the simulator), showing the two or three things that matter most.
2. Trim it to 15–30 seconds and export at your device's preview size (for example, 886×1920 for the 6.9-inch slot).
3. Keep any text short and in your app's own style.

For TikTok, X and Product Hunt, where App Review doesn't apply, make a motion-graphics cut with [PromoReel](/create?for=tiktok-app-promo): kinetic type, your real numbers counting up, your logo at the end.

## Tips

- **Lead with your best screen.** Many people only see the first few seconds.
- **One idea per scene.** A caption of two to five words about the thing on screen.
- **Make a separate social cut.** Use a louder style like [Y2K Chrome](/aesthetics/y2k-chrome) for [TikTok](/for/tiktok-app-promo), where App Review doesn't apply.
