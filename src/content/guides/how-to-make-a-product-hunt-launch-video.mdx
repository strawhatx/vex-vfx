**Short answer:** make a 16:9 video of 30–60 seconds that shows what your product does in the first three seconds, upload it to YouTube, and add the YouTube link as the first item in your Product Hunt gallery. You don't need a video editor: three to five good screenshots are enough.

## What Product Hunt does with your video

Product Hunt doesn't host video files. When you add media to your launch, you paste a YouTube link, and it plays inside your gallery. Put it first so it's the thing people see when they open your launch.

Most visitors watch with the sound off and decide within seconds whether to keep watching. So the video's job is narrow: make it obvious what the product is and that it looks good. Your tagline, description and first comment do the rest.

## The specs

| What         | Recommendation                            |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 (1920×1080)                          |
| Length       | 30–60 seconds                             |
| Hosting      | YouTube (unlisted works)                  |
| Sound        | Optional; captions should carry the story |
| Position     | First item in the gallery                 |

## A structure that works

1. **The hook (2 seconds).** One line with the outcome: "Changelogs that write themselves". Not your logo on its own.
2. **Three to five scenes.** One screenshot each, zooming into the one thing that matters on that screen. Skip settings pages, empty states and login screens.
3. **Captions as benefits.** "Ship notes in one click", not "Markdown export".
4. **The end card.** Your name and URL, held for a couple of seconds, so a screenshot of the last frame still tells people where to go.

## Making it in two minutes

1. Take 3–6 screenshots of your best screens. Phone or desktop both work.
2. Open [PromoReel](/create?for=product-hunt-launch-video), which presets 16:9 for Product Hunt.
3. Pick a style. [Clean Apple](/aesthetics/clean-apple) is a safe default; [Brutalist](/aesthetics/brutalist) stands out if your product has attitude.
4. Let AI direct it: copy the prompt into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with your screenshots, or use your own API key. Or let the built-in rules make a plan instantly.
5. Edit the captions, hit Re-direct if you want another take, and download the MP4.
6. Upload it to YouTube and paste the link into your Product Hunt gallery.

## Use the same video everywhere on launch day

Export a vertical 9:16 version as well (it's one toggle) and post it on [X](/for/x-launch-video), [TikTok](/for/tiktok-app-promo) and Reels. A video makes your launch post look finished, and people share finished things.

## Common mistakes

- **A long intro.** Every second before the product appears loses viewers.
- **Too many features.** Pick the three that make people say "oh, nice".
- **Tiny text.** If a caption can't be read on a phone, it doesn't exist.
- **No link at the end.** The last frame is the one people screenshot.
